Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy covers the Repertoire apps for iPhone, iPad, and Android, their hosted service, and this website.

What we collect and how we use it

  • Account and profile. We collect your email address, display name, and password when you create an account. We use these to create your account and sign you in. The server stores a salted password hash. On iPhone and iPad, the app keeps your sign-in token in Keychain. On Android, the app encrypts the token with a key stored in Android Keystore. You can also add an instrument, bio, and profile photo.
  • Music and practice. We store saved pieces, their statuses and rankings, and the piece, duration, and date of each saved practice session. The app uses your collection and preferences to recommend pieces. If you submit a missing piece, its catalogue information may be available to other musicians.
  • Social activity. We store follows, likes, repertoire updates, and invite code redemptions to provide the feed and manage access to Discover. During practice, the app sends the current piece and timer status so eligible followers can see that you’re playing.
  • Diagnostics and support. The service records aggregate request and database metrics to help troubleshoot performance. When you email support, we use your email address and the information you share to respond to your request.

What other musicians can see

Your name, instrument, bio, profile photo, and follow connections are visible to other signed-in musicians. Your repertoire is public by default. Other musicians can view a public repertoire, and eligible followers can see repertoire updates and active practice status. Scores from public repertoires may contribute to community ratings. Your saved practice history and account email stay private.

To hide your saved pieces and repertoire activity, open Profile → Edit profile, turn off Public repertoire, and tap Save. Your profile and follow connections remain visible.

Hosting and tracking

Render hosts the default Repertoire service and processes the data needed to run it. If you configure a different server, the app sends your account and repertoire data to that server. Render’s Privacy Policy

Support messages are handled through Gmail.

Repertoire does not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. This website shows screenshots with sample data and sets no cookies or analytics trackers. The website host may record standard request information such as your IP address.

Retention and your choices

We keep account and activity records while your account is active so your repertoire remains available when you sign in. You can edit your profile, remove saved pieces, unfollow musicians, or undo likes in the app.

To request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data, email cxy20052008@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may verify that you own the account before acting. Deleting your account removes your profile, saved repertoire, practice history, and associated social activity from the active service. Copies may remain in backups until those backups are removed.
