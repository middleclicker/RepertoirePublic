Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Your private journal and your public profile are stored separately.

This policy covers the Repertoire iPhone, iPad, and Android apps and website, maintained by the developer known as middleclicker. Contact cxy20052008@gmail.com about privacy, account access, or deletion.

Account and private cloud journal

Firebase Authentication links your account to a verified email address, Google sign-in, or Sign in with Apple. Authentication credentials are held in the Apple Keychain on iOS or encrypted with a non-exportable Android Keystore key on Android; passwords are not stored in your journal. Google and Apple process their own sign-in flows. Earlier anonymous identities may be linked to a verified account.

Your complete journal is automatically synchronized to private Firebase/Google Cloud storage: profile and appearance preferences, repertoire statuses, difficulty and enjoyment rankings and comparisons, custom pieces, composition years, personal cover photos, favorite passages, recording links, imported PDFs, and practice history including an active timer. Each signed-in device keeps an offline copy. The private journal is accessible only through authenticated account services or authorized operational support; it is not public and is not used as community rating evidence except through the separate eligible comparison contribution flow. Storage is encrypted by the provider, but is not end-to-end encrypted.

Sync uses file checksums, account-specific storage, short-lived file transfer links, and revision checks. Conflicting device changes require you to choose which complete journal to continue with. The previous cloud manifest and a local recovery copy may be retained for recovery. Unreferenced old cloud files are cleaned up during later syncs; interrupted uploads can remain temporarily. The current journal limit is 250 MB. Public score-download caches can be recreated and are not uploaded as private files. Device backups you enable through Apple may also include app data. Android system backup is disabled for the app. Android lets you explicitly export a ZIP backup of your private journal and PDFs to a location you choose; this backup is not encrypted by Repertoire, and the chosen storage provider may process it under its own terms. Keep exported backups private.

Public profiles and community

Profiles are public by default and have no private mode. Your name or nickname, instrument, stated experience level, bio, repertoire titles and statuses, shared favorite passage titles/bar ranges/notes, selected Loved pieces, profile picture, and any practice sessions you explicitly share may appear to other musicians. Updates publish automatically when community services are available. Use a nickname if preferred and avoid sharing sensitive information.

Personal numeric scores, individual comparison history, personal cover photos, private recording links, and imported PDFs are not included in the public profile. Follow relationships, public repertoire overlap, and eligible shared signals support feeds and recommendations. Community difficulty contributions send eligible comparisons, catalogue identifiers, experience level, familiarity, and revisions to estimate general difficulty. These are account-linked contributions separate from your full private journal. Referral codes, account creation date, inviter relationships, redemption, and qualifying counts are stored to manage access and prevent abuse. Explicit reviewer entitlements may unlock review accounts without adding referral credits or community votes.

Moderation and support

Public text is checked before publication. New profile pictures are sent to Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch to assess nudity, sexual content, and graphic violence before publication. Failed checks can prevent a new image from being shared; an approved image hash and check time are cached. Automated checks are imperfect and may be supplemented by human review. Blocks, reports, the reported content snapshot, account identifiers, and moderation actions are stored to handle abuse and appeals. Support messages are used to respond and investigate your request. See our Community rules.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide authentication, private file storage, Firestore database records, backend processing, and photo safety checks. Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers optional Android notifications. When enabled, Repertoire stores a private device registration token and notification preference, and sends notifications for followers, reactions, and friends’ practice activity. They process connection information and operational logs to deliver and secure these services. See Firebase privacy information and Google Cloud's privacy notice.
  • Apple processes Apple sign-in and push notifications. If enabled, Repertoire stores a private APNs device token and notification preference for follow alerts. Composer and piece-title queries are sent to iTunes to find representative recording artwork, along with connection information. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • External links open providers such as YouTube or official score sellers. Their policies apply when you visit them.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this static website and processes requests under GitHub's privacy statement. This website has no analytics scripts, cookies, forms, or browser storage.

Repertoire does not sell personal data, serve advertising, or use your activity for cross-app advertising tracking.

Your choices and deletion

Edit your profile, remove pieces and passages, unshare practice sessions, block musicians, and disable notifications in the app or your device Settings. Sign out to stop the account being used on that device; this preserves its local account copy. Signing out or uninstalling does not delete your account or cloud journal.

Profile → Delete account requires recent sign-in and removes Firebase identity, public profile and pictures, relationships, inbox/device records, community contributions, referrals, private cloud journal/files, and account files on the current device. Interrupted deletion can be retried. Private file transfers issued just before deletion expire within 15 minutes; a delayed cleanup removes any late uploads after 20 minutes, with retries if infrastructure is temporarily unavailable. Copies already exported or downloaded to other devices cannot be remotely erased; sign out or delete local copies there as well. Provider backups and operational logs may follow separate retention schedules.

For access, correction, or deletion requests when you cannot use the app, contact cxy20052008@gmail.com. We verify account ownership before providing private data or deleting an account. Do not send passwords or tokens.

Retention and security

Account and journal data are retained while your account is active. Recovery copies remain until cleaned up, replaced, or the account is deleted. Pseudonymous moderation audit records and limited operational records may remain for security, abuse prevention, and legal obligations. Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Repertoire uses HTTPS, platform-protected credentials, private storage, and server-side account authorization; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We update this policy when data practices change and revise the effective date. Contact cxy20052008@gmail.com with questions.
