Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Your musical life is personal. Here’s what stays on your device, what’s shared, and how to manage it.

This policy covers the Repertoire iPhone and iPad app and this website, maintained by the developer at middleclicker.

On your device

Repertoire saves your journal on your device: your pieces and their status, personal difficulty and enjoyment ratings, comparison history, favorite excerpts, profile preferences, and appearance settings. Core catalogue search and personal rankings work offline. Your full journal is not uploaded as a cloud backup by Repertoire. Device backups you enable through Apple may include app data.

Public profiles and community features

Profiles are public by default. When the online service is configured, completing setup or using an existing journal publishes your name or nickname, instrument, experience level, bio, saved piece titles and statuses, favorite excerpt bar ranges and notes, and selected Loved pieces. Updates are shared automatically. There is no private-profile switch. Avoid putting sensitive information in your profile or excerpt notes.

Personal numeric ratings, individual comparison history, and saved recording URLs are not included in your public profile. Follow relationships and repertoire overlap support feeds, musician suggestions, and recommendations.

Community difficulty features can automatically send eligible piano comparisons, catalogue identifiers, your stated experience level, and answer revisions to the backend. These contributions are associated with an anonymous service identifier and used to estimate community difficulty. Your public profile is separate from this contribution data.

Where referral features are available, the service stores an account-linked referral code, account creation date, referral redemption and inviter relationship, and qualifying referral count to manage access and prevent abuse.

Online services

  • Google Firebase provides anonymous authentication, database storage, and backend processing. An anonymous identifier connects your service activity; it does not mean that your activity is unlinked. Credentials are stored in the device Keychain. Service providers also process connection information, such as IP addresses and operational logs, to deliver and secure the service. See Firebase’s privacy information.
  • Apple receives composer and piece-title searches for representative album artwork through iTunes, along with the connection information needed to serve searches and images. If you enable push notifications, Repertoire stores an APNs device token and notification preference to send follow alerts. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • External recording links open the provider you choose, such as YouTube. That provider’s privacy policy applies when you visit it.

Repertoire does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics, sell your personal data, or use your activity for cross-app advertising tracking.

Your choices and deletion

Edit your profile and remove individual pieces or excerpts in the app. Disable notifications in iOS Settings. Use Profile → Reset app to delete your public profile, incoming and outgoing follow relationships, your notification inbox and registered push devices, then clear your local journal and settings. The app requires a successful online profile deletion before completing this reset. Completing setup again can publish a new profile.

Reset is not full service-account deletion. The anonymous authentication identity, community contributions, referral records, and operational records may remain. Removing the app alone does not delete your public profile or server data. Copies you export or save outside the app remain under your control.

For access, correction, or deletion of remaining server data, or if you can no longer access the app, email cxy20052008@gmail.com. We may need information to locate your records and verify that they are yours; anonymous accounts may be difficult to identify after device access is lost. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

Retention and security

Local data remains until you remove or reset it. Server records are retained to operate community features, maintain the integrity of contributions and referrals, and prevent abuse, unless removed through the applicable deletion process. There is no single automatic deletion period for all server data. Providers’ logs and backups may follow their own retention schedules. Services may process data outside your country.

Repertoire uses HTTPS for online requests, device Keychain storage for credentials, and authenticated backend access. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

This website and contact

This website uses no analytics scripts, forms, cookies, or browser storage. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process IP addresses and request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you contact us, we use your message and contact information to respond and handle your request.

For privacy questions or support, email cxy20052008@gmail.com or visit the issue page. Issues are public; use email for personal information or deletion requests.

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the effective date above.
