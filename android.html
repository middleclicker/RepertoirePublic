How to install

  1. Download the APK on your Android device and open it from your browser’s downloads or the Files app.
  2. If Android prompts you, open Settings and enable Allow from this source for the browser or Files app you used.
  3. Return to the installer and tap Install. You can turn off the source permission after installation.
