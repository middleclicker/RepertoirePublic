Read and play along with your score, plan what to practice, and record takes to hear how you’re progressing. Sign in with your existing Repertoire account to see your saved pieces and practice history.

Version 1.0.1 · Android 9 or newer · 53 MB

What’s new in 1.0.1

  • Sheet music. Open a catalogue score or import MusicXML, including compressed .mxl files. Play from a bar, loop a passage, or turn your phone sideways. For a multi-part piece, show your part and adjust the others’ volume.
  • Practice plans. Set a weekly goal with reminders or build a bar-by-bar timeline for a deadline. Use the timer, metronome, or tuner, then save the bars you covered and listen back to your takes.
  • Discover and profiles. Follow musicians, listen to shared recordings, and invite mutual followers to practice with you. Share your own profile with a QR code.

How to install

  1. Download the APK on your Android device and open it from your browser’s downloads or the Files app.
  2. If Android prompts you, open Settings and enable Allow from this source for the browser or Files app you used.
  3. Return to the installer and tap Install or Update. You can turn off the source permission afterward.
