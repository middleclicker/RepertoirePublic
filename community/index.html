Community rules

Effective October 18, 2026

A welcoming place for musicians at every level.

Respect other musicians

No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, discrimination, sexual exploitation, sexually explicit content, or graphic violence. Do not publish another person's private information. Musical experience and rankings are personal; do not target someone for their ability or preferences.

Share honestly

No impersonation, spam, scams, manipulated referrals, or coordinated attempts to falsify community ratings. Do not present demo or reviewer accounts as independent rating evidence. Give composers, arrangers, performers, and editors appropriate credit.

Respect copyright

Share only content you have permission to share. Private PDF imports stay in your account. Do not use profile text, passages, or external links to distribute unauthorized scores or recordings. For copyright complaints, email cxy20052008@gmail.com with the affected content, your rights, and a way to contact you.

Filtering and reporting

Public profile text is checked for prohibited terms and threats before publication. New profile photos undergo automated nudity and violence checks. Automated checks are imperfect; they do not replace human review. In a musician's public profile, use Report to tell us about inappropriate content or Block to stop interaction. You may also report any profile, passage, or other public content by emailing cxy20052008@gmail.com; include the profile name and the content or a screenshot, without sending passwords or sign-in tokens.

What happens next

The developer reviews reports and may remove or hide content, restrict an account, or permanently ban serious or repeated abuse. We aim to respond to reports within 24 hours and prioritize threats, exploitation, and immediate safety concerns. Email cxy20052008@gmail.com for urgent reports, a blocked upload that appears mistaken, or an appeal. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Public sharing and privacy

Profiles are public by default and cannot be made private. Use a nickname if you prefer. Never put sensitive personal details in your bio or shared passage notes. Your private journal, imported PDFs, and personal numeric scores are not part of your public profile. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
