Common questions

What if Repertoire can’t connect?

Check your internet connection and tap Retry. If you use your own server, make sure it’s running and check its address under Server settings on the connection screen.

How do I make my repertoire private?

Open Profile → Edit profile, turn off Public repertoire, and tap Save. Other musicians can still see your profile and follow connections.

How do I unlock Discover?

Open Discover to find your invite code. Once a new musician signs up with your code or enters it in their account within 14 days of signing up, tap Check invite status.

How do I delete my account?

Email cxy20052008@gmail.com from the address associated with your Repertoire account and request deletion. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.
